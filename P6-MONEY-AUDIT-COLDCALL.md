## 3. Round ids and refs
- `rec.id = crypto.randomBytes(6).toString('hex')` (cc:323, legacy cc:472): 48 bits, never checked for collision (`open` Map keyed by it, cc:57).
- Survives a restart only inside the open record (`roundId`, cc:292, st:69); used as `ref.round` of the restart refund (cc:307). Settled ids live in memory history (cc:252) and, for Chips, in ledger rows.
- Refs passed: spend `{game:'coldcall', round}` (cc:340, 480); credit win `{game, round}` (cc:205, 245); prize `{...ref, pot:true}` (246); refund `{game, round, void: why}` (285), `void:'restart'` (307).
- wallet.js reads from `ref` only: `game` (stats, w:84, 98) and `round` (ledger row, w:60-61, Chips only because `logPlay` is false for Play $, w:58, ix:24). `ref.void` and `ref.pot` are ignored. wallet.js never remembers a ref: crediting the same ref twice credits twice (w:91-103).

## 4. Timing (which steps sit in different ticks)
- One socket message, one sync block: `spin` (458) incl. `pullSpin`, settle of a non-pending round. `decide` (410) and `ready` (441) are later messages. The spend and the open record are in the spin message; the win credit is in a LATER tick (decide message, timer, or disconnect event).
- `setTimeout` that later moves money: `setTimer` (cc:185-188) -> `autoSettle` -> `settle` credits, or `voidRound` credits. Armed at 180,000 ms by `armTimer` (175-181, `CEILING_MS` cc:36); the first `ready` shortens it to `timeoutMs` (default 20,000, 3,000..120,000 live, PE:section 8 table) (454). Re-armed by every decide (431) and in `ready` (454).
- Debounce timers that decide what is on disk: w:36, st:43 (50 ms, `unref`). Explicit flushes: cc:241, 247, 283, 286, 306, 310, 349, 350. Exit hooks only on normal exit (w:15, st:10). SIGTERM handler `shutdownCashOut` (srv:1868): whether it flushes the wallet or the store: not verified.
- Things an adapter that flushes at the END of the tick would break: the code relies on `spend` throwing `funds` synchronously (340, walletErr 46), on `credit` returning the new balance at once (245-246, `w` goes into the result), on the pot file being flushed BEFORE the prize credit (241 vs 246), on the open record being flushed AFTER the spend (349-350), on `voidRound` / `voidStored` deleting the record before crediting (283, 306), and on `wallet.get` right after (246, 384). `pushWallet` already batches per microtask (ix:14-22) but that is display only.

## 5. The pot in detail
- Per paid spin (spin or buy; not Callback, cc:212), at SETTLE time: `n = cost * feedBps + rem`; `slice = floor(n/10000)`; `rem = n % 10000` (en:175). Shipped `feedBps` 100 = 1% (en:87). The fraction stays in `pot.rem` (cc:231).
- Hit: one `potRng()` draw per paid settle (cc:215, not on the round's tape), chance `min(1, cost/100/oneInPerDollar)` (en:178; shipped 3000). Independent of pot size (PS:31). Needs `bal(after slice) >= minBal` (1000) and `bal > 0` (cc:216).
- Prize: `min(bal, capCents)` (en:183), capCents 5000 for every bet; the remainder stays in the pot. Outside the 10,000x win cap (PS:30, "keep it" recommendation; not verified as final).
- Seed: shipped 0, live config refuses non-zero (lc:110). With seed > 0 it would be money from nowhere ("tracked house money", st:56, cc:234). Today the pot holds only slices of player costs.
- `fed + seeded = paid + bal`: written at st:4 and PE section 4; checked at runtime NOWHERE. `validPot` checks five safe non-negative integers only (st:15-17); a pot entry failing it is silently replaced by an empty one (st:28, 62). The only check is the test `potOk` (coldcall-pull-server.js:102).
- Per currency: yes, `pot.play` and `pot.chips` (st:57-59, cc:122-123).
- Another player's settle changes the pot between my spin and my settle: yes. My round reads `pot` at settle (cc:210) and mutates it in one sync block (230-239), so I see the other's slice and prize. The slice uses MY `cfg` snapshot (cc:213: `rec.cfg`); `bal` and `rem` are live. Two settles in one tick cannot both take the pot (sync, tested at coldcall-pull-server.js:356).

## 6. Live config
- Per round: `rec.K = L.snapshot()` = `structuredClone(Eng.CFG)` + an engine built from it (cc:327, lc:234); `rec.cfg = K.cfg.pull` (cc:328). Pay table, prices, pot knobs (feedBps, minBal, oneInPerDollar, capCents, seed), feed knobs and the decision timer all come from the snapshot (cc:211-222, 176). Price and cost of the NEXT spin come from the new live config.
- Open round at a swap: pays exactly its own snapshot, slice booked at its own `feedBps` (PE section 8). Stored player state is repaired per field by `normState` (cc:73-91); a damaged field resets that currency's whole state = leads and Callback gone (cc:89, PE section 8 item c). Leads/Callback are valued in cents-of-bet at the time they were earned, so a price change does not re-value them.
- Restart: config reloaded from coldcall-config.json (cc:371, lc:195); a damaged file = shipped numbers + one log line. Open rounds are voided and refunded at `init`, never replayed (cc:377; PE section 8). The open record carries `cfg` (cc:292) but `voidStored` uses only `cost`.
- Pot cap vs feed: capCents >= feedBps x oneInPerDollar / 100 is enforced (lc:112); a live change of any pot knob applies to the next settle of any round on a new snapshot, and to the pot's existing `bal` at once (cap and minBal are read from the settling round's snapshot).

## 8. Not ledger balances / not atomic (worst first)
1. Open round: stake in wallet.json/bank.json, open record in coldcall-pull.json, flushed one after the other (cc:349-350). Crash between = stake kept by the house, no refund.
2. Settle of an open round flushes the state and drops the record (241) before the win credit (245). Crash between, or a swallowed credit error = the player loses stake and win.
3. Pot prize: pot file says `paid += prize` before the prize credit (241, 246). Crash between = prize destroyed, pot invariant still "holds".
4. Restart refund (306-310) and `voidRound` (283-286) delete the record first, credit second, wallet flushed after the loop. Crash = refund lost.
5. A corrupt or unparseable coldcall-pull.json (st:30) empties every player state, pot AND open record: open stakes never refunded, pots reset to 0, leads and Callbacks gone. A single invalid pot entry resets that pot (st:28).
6. The pot is a number in coldcall-pull.json, not an account. Its slice never leaves any balance (cost is already a sink, cc:340); `fed+seeded=paid+bal` is never checked at runtime (st:4 only).
7. Unpaid win (bank / shown cents) of an open round exists only as a replayable tape in memory (cc:150-163); at a restart the player gets the cost back (307), not the shown bank. Real effect: a player sitting on a shown 200x bonus gets 1x back.
8. Leads, `avg`, `carry`, `cb`, `warm` are stored in coldcall-pull.json only, worth cents but never in a balance; daily leads (en:535-538) and a reset by `normState` (cc:89) change their value with no money entry.
9. Immediate rounds write the wallet and the state/pot in two separate debounced writers (w:36, st:43); `flush` only when `wasOpen || potWon` (241). A crash in the 50 ms window can replay a consumed Callback or lose a win.
10. Win and prize are two `credit` calls (245-246); a throw in either is swallowed (logged only) so the pot/state already advanced.
11. Top-up and admin set SET the balance (w:123, w:112): no ref, no ledger row, allowed while a round is open; top-up mints `START_PLAY - play`.
12. wallet.js counts every credit with `ref.game` as `won` (w:98): void refunds and pot prizes inflate `won`; Callback rounds never raise `rounds` (no spend, w:84).
13. No idempotency anywhere: wallet.js ignores `ref` for dedupe (w:91-103), round ids are random and unchecked (cc:323), a resent `spin` is a new round.
14. Both debounced writers swallow write errors (w:34, st:41): memory silently runs ahead of disk until the next successful write.
15. Chips are split from the wallet: the Chips balance lives in bank.json via `adjustBank` (clamps at 0, srv:144), written per call, while stats stay in wallet.json; ledger rows exist only for Chips (w:58, ix:24).
16. Live config (prices, pot cap, feed rate) lives in coldcall-config.json (lc:164), outside any balance; it moves house exposure at once for the next round (lc:170-174).
