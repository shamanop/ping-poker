- Appointment leaf (bottom-left lip) and office-pot mug (bottom-right lip) flank the WIN window; feed rides the rule bar's right end (rotates 3 beats line / 1 beat "MAX 10,000X"; own events are a "YOU" tag; hidden while a prompt or a Callback / warm warning owns the bar).
- PICK: the rule bar becomes the prompt ("PICK YOUR LEAD 0:14") and drains; the lit squares get a gold ring (turns red under 5 s). Squares are the tap targets (64 stage px = 43 px on a 360 screen).

(pWin from the pending payload, to 2 decimals trimmed, the two always add to 100; Chips mode appends "chips"). The drain bar is a slim bar on top of the keys plus the rule bar (no dial between the keys). Balance / bet / SPIN are hidden for the decision; the no-payouts line and the BUY / AUTO / TURBO row stay.
- GHOST (steal 2): a yellow sticky with a double-border rubber stamp "WOULD HAVE CLOSED $12.40 / if a phone had landed. Not paid" in the head band (the bubble steps aside); the lit squares flip to the quote bubbles of `ghost.script`; the WIN window is untouched. Tap skips; it never resolves a decision.
- Warm squares (idle, bet = warmBet): amber lit squares + a red price stamp with the bet; at any other bet the stamps go and the rule bar warns "4 WARM LEADS WORK AT $1.00 ONLY" (squares are dropped by game.js `syncWarm`).
- CALLBACK: SPIN reads CALLBACK (game.js) with a gold glow, bet label "FREE BONUS AT", rule bar gold "FREE BONUS AT $2.00", sticky reads FULL.
- POT win: medallion in #scene (mug, cash wads, count-up, "on top of your win. Outside the 10,000x cap."). ONE MORE CALL result: a sticky "YOU DOUBLED IT" / "NO DEAL" + round total. Lead gain: "+1.2 LEADS" tag on the sticky, then head-band stickies (went cold, APPOINTMENT KEPT +N leads day S, CALLBACK!), each 1.2 s, total under the 4 s A allows.
- Info screen: `CC.pull.infoLines()`; a MutationObserver on #ov adds "The pull" (one honest line per mechanic, numbers from `rules` else `E.CFG.pull`, the pot odds `1 in oneInPerDollar per $1 bet`, "the pot is extra, on top of the 10,000x cap") inside `.card.info`. No change to game.js needed.
- Honesty: every number is from the view / pending payload / rules / ghost script / `ctx.cents`. No 50 / 400 anywhere (list from `view.list`).
- Motion: no looping animation. The drain bars are redrawn by a 100 ms timer; one-shot pops are skipped under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Turbo / ghost gating stays in game.js.

## ASK A (relay)
1. **Stale local timer after a long animation (reproduced, bonus2 `more`, 2 of 2 runs on 4640).** `decisionPoint` sends `T.ready(id)` but `ctx.timer.expiresAt` still comes from the pending result's receipt time; a bonus animation of 20 s or more means `ctx.timer.left() <= 0` when the prompt opens, the 150 ms `expiry` interval fires at once ("TIME'S UP: banked", decisions `['player','timeout']`) before the server's `g:coldcall:timer` reply lands. Fix in game.js, line ~237 next to `T.ready(id)`: `const t = ctx.timer; t.at = Date.now(); t.expiresAt = t.at + (t.timeoutMs || 20000);` (then the server reply re-syncs it). My prompt (pick and more) reads `ctx.timer.left()` / `.timeoutMs` every 100 ms, so it follows.
2. Nothing else needed: `P('setView'...)`, `onBetChange`, `feed`, `setPot`, `rules`, `timer`, `expired`, `askPick`, `askMore`, `moreOutcome`, `potWin`, `ghost`, `leadGain` are all called as in PULL-UI.md section 5. `ctx` fields I use: `cents, bet, p.totalWinTenths, timer, pickTargets, wait, tween, waitTap, SFX, FX`.
3. FYI for the lead: `ctx.promptOpen` is my watchdog's "round still wants a prompt" flag (my prompt self-closes within ~0.5 s if `st.ctx` changes or `promptOpen` goes null: abortRound / voided leave nothing behind).
