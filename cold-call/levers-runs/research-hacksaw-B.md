Pattern across the five "Le" games (from the pages above; the grouping is my reading, not a published rule): the ladder is a 3x BonusHunt (all five, "5 times more likely" trigger), then a 30-80x entry tier (Viking 30x, Bandit 50x, Fisherman 50x, Pharaoh 60x, Zeus 50x/80x), a 100x tier in four of five, and a 200-500x top tier (Viking 200x, Bandit 250x, Pharaoh 250x, Zeus 300x, Fisherman 500x). The cheap 3x BonusHunt has the same RTP as the 100x+ buys: the price is scaled to trigger frequency, not to a different payback.

### 2.1 Regulatory restrictions (UK, Sweden, other)

UK (UK Gambling Commission, Remote Gambling and Software Technical Standards, in force 31 October 2021), primary source https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/standards/remote-gambling-and-software-technical-standards/rts-14-responsible-product-design :

- RTS 14D, quote: "It must be a minimum of 2.5 seconds from the time a game is started until the next game cycle can be commenced. It must always be necessary to release and then depress the 'start button' or take equivalent action to commence a game cycle."
- RTS 14D guidance, quote: "A player should commit to each game cycle individually, continued contact with a button, key or screen should not initiate a new game cycle." (this is why autoplay and hold-to-spin are out; it is stated in the same RTS page, which also has an "RTS 8 - Auto-play functionality" section header)
- RTS 14E, quote: "The gambling system must not permit a customer to reduce the time until the result is presented." Guidance: "Features such as turbo, quick spin and slam stop are not permitted ... This applies to all remote games, regardless of game cycle speed. This requirement does not apply to bonus and/or feature games where an additional stake is not wagered."
- RTS 14F, quote: "The gambling system must not celebrate a return which is less than or equal to the total stake gambled." Guidance: win-associated "auditory or visual effects" are not permitted for such returns. Permitted instead: "Display of total amount awarded", short winning-line display, "Brief sound to indicate the result".
- UKGC consultation response on turbo, quote: "allowing customers to skip animation after the result has been communicated is permissible", "although as per new requirement 14D, the next game cycle cannot be started until the 2.5 seconds have elapsed." https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/consultation-response/online-games-design-and-reverse-withdrawals/summary-of-responses-prohibiting-player-led-spin-stop-features ; implementation date quoted there: "This requirement will come into force on 31 October 2021."
- Autoplay: banned in the UK per secondary sources, "Autoplay removed, and turbo, quick-spin and slam-stop features prohibited" https://www.olbg.com/slots/articles/bonus-buy-slots ; the dedicated UKGC autoplay response page was not fetched (page link exists: "Prohibiting auto-play functionality for online slots").
- Bonus buy in UK: OLBG states "bonus buys aren't available to British players" and "Not at a UKGC-licensed casino. The buy bonus feature is deactivated in UK versions of these games" and dates the removal to October 2021 https://www.olbg.com/slots/articles/bonus-buy-slots . I did not find the buy ban in the RTS text I fetched (14D to 14F cover speed and celebration, not buy); treat the UKGC-text basis as not confirmed, the market fact (no buy in UK builds) as confirmed by multiple sources (also https://www.olbg.com/slots/games/san-quentin-xways: "Feature buy-in is not available at UKGC-licensed casinos").
- Net effect for UK builds: no buy, no autoplay, no turbo/quick spin, 2.5 s floor per spin, no win celebration on returns up to stake (loss-disguised-as-win ban).

Sweden: no bonus-buy ban found. Sources found are a Play'n GO survey ("55% of Swedish slot players think 'Bonus Buy' games should be banned") and discussion only: https://www.playngo.com/post/swedish-slot-players-think-bonus-buy-games-should-be-banned . Spelinspektionen rules on spin speed, autoplay: not published in anything fetched. (Hacksaw's Sweden items found were licensing fines, https://next.io/news/regulation/swedish-court-reduces-sga-fine-hacksaw-studios/ , not design rules.)

Netherlands: "Bonus Buy games are banned by ... KSA of the Netherlands and the UK Gambling Commission" (summary of https://www.playngo.com/post/swedish-slot-players-think-bonus-buy-games-should-be-banned , not independently verified).

### 3.4 Nolimit conventions that bear on payback shape

- xNudge: wild nudges into view, +1 multiplier per step (Tombstone RIP, Mental). Win multiplier lives on the wild, not on the total. https://www.bigwinboard.com/tombstone-2-nolimit-city-slot-review/
- xWays: reveals a matching symbol stacked 4 high (8 high from two cells); xSplit / Razor Split: halve symbols on a reel so every position count doubles. https://www.olbg.com/slots/games/san-quentin-xways
- xBet / Double xBet: documented on San Quentin 2: Death Row (not on the two flagships above): "By using the xBet option, you have a 1 in 100 spins chance of triggering the Green Mile Spins feature as well as a higher hit frequency of 30%. ... Double xBet ... every 10 spins with a hit frequency rate of nearly 50%". Cost multipliers not in that snippet: not published. Source: WebSearch summary of https://casino.betmgm.com/en/blog/game-reviews/san-quentin-xways-slot-review/ and https://www.olbg.com/slots/games/san-quentin-2-death-row (OLBG page confirms Green Mile Spins "every 299 spins", RTP 96.13%, hit frequency 26.9%, max win 200,000x, "1 in 1,525,786 spins"). Tombstone RIP's equivalent is "Enhanced Bet" +10% cost, 96.28% RTP.
- Hit-frequency suppression via xRIP: wins below the base bet are voided (Tombstone RIP). The default RTP is unchanged; the spread is moved into rarer, larger wins.
- Buy RTPs sit above the headline RTP and rise with price: San Quentin 96.26 / 96.26 / 96.95 vs 96.03 headline (+0.23, +0.23, +0.92); Tombstone 96.47 / 97.03 vs 96.08 (+0.39, +0.95). Sources in 3.1 and 3.2. Contrast with Hacksaw: Bandit buys 96.28-96.40 vs 96.34 headline (-0.06 to +0.06).

### 4.4 Pragmatic conventions that bear on payback shape

- Ante bet: +25% stake, scatter odds doubled, buy disabled while it is on (Sweet Bonanza, Gates). Price +25% for 2x trigger frequency; the RTP shift is not published for Gates (Sweet Bonanza ante 96.51 vs 96.48 per a low-quality fan site). Whether the bonus is under- or over-priced at 25%: estimate (not computable without the bonus trigger odds, which are not published). https://www.bigwinboard.com/sweet-bonanza-pragmatic-play-online-slot/ ; https://www.bigwinboard.com/gates-of-olympus-pragmatic-play-slot-review/
- Tumble multiplier conventions: (a) random multiplier symbols 2x-500x summed and applied at the end of a tumble chain (Gates, Sweet Bonanza); (b) global running multiplier that never resets in free spins (Gates); (c) grid-position multipliers that double on every re-hit, x2 up to x128, cleared in base, kept in bonus (Sugar Rush).
