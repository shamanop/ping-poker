## Behaviour changes worth knowing (P4b)
- The bank panel and admin console edit the BANK balance (delta), not a total; chips at the table are separate.
- Rebuy: the panel no longer closes on click; it closes when the server seats the chips.
- A Raise/Bet whose box is invalid sends nothing (no clamp-and-send); typing the whole stack asks for a tap to confirm.
- The rail is wider (212px min) and stickers/throws use 4 columns.
- Toasts appear in the stage corner (right; left when the panel is on the right).
- "Pause" in the header is gone; the player button reads Sit out / Back in.
